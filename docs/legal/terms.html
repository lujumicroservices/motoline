The product

RiderLab is a motorcycle companion that records GPS/IMU data and provides post-ride analysis. Coaching tips and scores are algorithmic estimates for practice — not professional instruction, race timing, or insurance telematics.

Safety

  • Do not interact with the phone while riding. Mount it securely or use arm-to-pocket flows before you roll.
  • Obey traffic laws. Lean and brake metrics are phone-derived proxies and can be wrong.
  • We do not incentivize dangerous riding. Ignore any tip that conflicts with safety or local law.
