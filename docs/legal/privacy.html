Privacy Policy

RiderLab by RawThrottle · Last updated: 24 August 2026

RiderLab records motorcycle rides on your phone so you can review the line you took and improve technique. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Location (precise, including background while a ride is active) — to draw your GPS line with the screen off or phone in a pocket. We do not sell location data.
  • Motion sensors — accelerometer / gyroscope (and barometer when present) for lean and ride quality metrics stored with your track.
  • Account — optional Google Sign-In for a durable profile, friends, and cloud sync. Guest mode uses an anonymous cloud session.
  • Ride content you create — tracks, titles, share visibility, optional photos and radio messages on group rides (rodadas), and labels you submit to help train the product.
  • Reports — if you flag a message or photo, we store the report, who sent it, and enough of the content for staff to review.
  • Device / diagnostics — app version and soft-fail sync/error events so we can fix crashes (not advertising profiles).

How we use it

  • Show your rides on-device (offline-first SQLite) and, when you sync, in your cloud garage.
  • Friends, compare, and rodadas — only with people you connect to and rides you choose to share.
  • Review reports of radio messages and photos so we can hide abuse and ban accounts that break the rules.
  • Improve lean sensing and coaching using labels you explicitly submit.

Where it is stored

On your device by default. Cloud sync uses Supabase (hosted Postgres). Processing may occur in the United States or other regions where our providers operate.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share only with infrastructure providers needed to run the app (e.g. Supabase, Google Sign-In, and — when enabled — RevenueCat / store billing), and when you choose Friends / Everyone visibility or join a rodada.

Retention & deletion

Local rides remain until you delete them or delete your account in the app. Cloud rides and profile data follow your account.

To delete your account and associated cloud data: open RiderLab → Settings → Account → Delete account, or follow the steps at Delete account / Eliminar cuenta. After deletion, account data is removed from active systems; infrastructure backups may retain residual copies for up to 30 days, then purge. We do not offer partial cloud data deletion without deleting the account.

Children

RiderLab is intended for adults who ride motorcycles (typically 18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.
