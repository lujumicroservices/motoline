Finding 1

Vertical tank or pocket → fused roll

Gravity sits on +Y (up the screen). Bike lean is left/right in that frame = roll. Gyro fusion keeps it alive through vibration.

Pitch is nod, hill, braking — not the corner.

Finding 2

Horizontal on the tank → pitch + vector

Gravity sits on +Z (screen up). The same bike lean is now a different phone axis. Portrait roll goes quiet. Pitch and vector follow the lean.

This is why three phones on a wall disagreed: some were on roll, one was on pitch.

The formula

Magnitude from vector. Sign from the winner.

bikeLean = sign(winning fused Euler) × angle(g, g0)
  • g0 Frozen gravity at upright (Layer A)
  • g Live gravity while riding
  • |∠| Vector lean — matches a clinometer
  • ± Left/right from roll (vertical) or pitch (flat)
Layer A · freeze

One ritual: upright, in the real mount, freeze g0

The app reads which phone axis is up. That is the pose. No corner needed. Mount chips are labels for training — sensors pick the channel.

Layer A · tank

Phone already mounted → Hold upright 4s

  • 1 Bike upright, not on the sidestand if that leans it
  • 2 Phone already portrait, landscape, or flat on the tank
  • 3 Hands off · tap Sostener vertical 4s
  • 4 Check banner: Vertical / Landscape / Flat · vector ~0°
  • 5 If vector is already 8°+, redo the freeze
Layer A · pocket

You cannot tap the screen in the pocket

  • 1 Sit on the bike, upright
  • 2 Tap Guardar en el bolsillo (still in hand)
  • 3 Pocket it fully before the countdown ends
  • 4 Stay still. App ignores the motion, then captures ~3s
  • 5 Strong haptic = freeze done. Ride can auto-start

Never hold 4s in front of your face — that freezes the hand, not the pocket.

Never

These break g0

  • Freeze in the hand, then pocket it
  • Freeze on the sidestand
  • Freeze while walking to the bike
  • Flip screen or change pocket after freeze
  • Trust roll when the banner says Flat
Layer B · while riding

Four channels tell us if the phone slipped

Vector lean is the oracle (always 3D tilt from g0). Every ~1s we ask: which Euler follows vector — and GPS turn rate?

Winner = highest |corr| × energy
Need |corr| > 0.55
Straight road → keep last pose
Sustained disagree → reclassify

Gyro holds the winning Euler through bumps. GPS is not lean — it only votes “we are in a curve.”
